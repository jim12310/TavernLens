Game-state reader provenance

HearthMirror.dll and untapped-scry-dotnet.dll are unmodified binaries supplied with the official HearthSim Hearthstone Deck Tracker v1.55.6 release:
https://github.com/HearthSim/Hearthstone-Deck-Tracker/releases/tag/v1.55.6

Newtonsoft.Json.dll and System.Reflection.DispatchProxy.dll are the matching dependency versions supplied in that release. Their respective ownership and license terms remain applicable. TavernLens does not claim authorship of these components.

The integration calls GetSceneMgrState, GetSelectedBattlegroundsGameMode and GetBattlegroundRatingInfo. It does not request collection, account identity, friends or other account data. No game writes are issued by this integration.
